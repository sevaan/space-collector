# Onboarding pitches (2026-10-06, parked)

Not built yet. Sevaan asked to park these and come back after the daytime experience.

## A. "First Night": a guided first outing
1. Welcome (hand of cards): "Let's catch your first one tonight."
2. Location, one line of why: "So the sky matches where you're standing."
3. Motion, demonstrated: tap, and the sky starts moving as you tilt (iOS needs this from a tap).
4. Calibrate: "Find the brightest star you can see." Point them at it, then drag the sky slider until the screen matches.
5. Guaranteed first catch: steer to the Moon, a planet or a bright star, so the reveal happens in ~2 minutes.
6. Hook: "Next satellite in 4 minutes, look NE." Tonight introduces itself by being useful.
Risk: needs a clear night; daytime/cloudy needs a fallback.

## B. "Your eyes are the instrument": dark adaptation
Eyes take 20–30 min to fully dark-adapt (roughly twice the stars). Offer red night mode up front (white light resets
your eyes). Note when they set the slider; nudge at ~5, 15, 30 min: "Your eyes have adjusted more, nudge the slider."
"Night Vision" achievement for staying out 30 min. A layer, not a whole onboarding.

## C. "The Training Card": daytime tutorial
A scripted ISS crosses a drawn sky to teach aim + catch. NOT added to the collection (no practice mode); ends with
"Now catch the real one: next visible pass Thu 8:12 PM · Remind me". Closest thing to practice mode, keep it a demo.

## D. "Mission Control briefing": theme wrapper
3–4 retro poster cards; sign on as a Stargazer, get your first missions; permissions as "equipment coming online".
Charming but skippable; use as tone, not content.

## Recommendation
A wrapped in D's voice (one line per step), B running quietly afterwards, C only as the daytime/cloudy fallback.
Every step skippable, replayable from Settings; returning users never see it.
