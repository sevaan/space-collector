Questions

Before you look up.

Do I need a telescope?

No. Everything in the game can be seen from the ground, most of it with your eyes alone. Binocular mode, free in Settings, adds fainter things like Neptune.

Does it work in the city?

Yes. Set how bright your sky is with the slider and the app only shows what you can actually see. The Moon, planets, bright stars and the brightest satellites show up even downtown.

What if it's cloudy?

The app checks the forecast, tells you the next clear night, and only nudges you when it's clear. If you spot a gap in the cloud, anything you can see still counts.

What does it do with my location?

Your approximate location works out your sky. A rounded version fetches your weather and nearby planes, and it's never sold. Your collection stays on your phone unless you sign in to back it up.

Is it really free?

Every card, patch and mission is free, with no ads. Plus is an optional extra for keen collectors.
