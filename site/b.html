Plate 2 · Field keyRarity is difficulty, not luck

How rare is it?

Each satellite is scored on how bright it gets, how often it passes over the places people live, how much of the night it spends in sunlight, and how long it has been up there.

RarityWhat it meansFor example
●CommonUp most clear nights. Bright stars, the Moon, the Sun.The Moon, Betelgeuse

◆RareFewer, dimmer or briefer passes.Hubble, the Space Station
✦EpicFaint, far, or seldom over your part of the world.The Andromeda Galaxy, Uranus
