How it works

Point. Tap. Collect.

No telescope and no tickets. Your phone already knows what's overhead from where you stand.

01 · Point

Hold your phone up

The sky on your screen lines up with the real one. Satellites drift across it with their paths drawn ahead of them.

02 · Tap

Catch it in the circle

When something moves into the circle it turns orange. Tap Collect before it slips away, and the card flips open.

03 · Keep

Build your collection

Every card carries real numbers: how high it flies, how fast, and the story behind it. Clear nights level your cards up.

Shinies

Catch the fade.

Satellites shine because the Sun still lights them after dark. Watch one slide into Earth's shadow and it vanishes mid-sky. Tap in the last seconds before it goes dark and the card comes out Shiny.

  • ☾Beside the Moon. Catch something passing close to it.
  • ↑Straight overhead. Catch it at the top of the sky.
  • ✦Just launched. Catch a satellite in its first 30 days.
  • ○Full Moon. Catch anything under a full Moon.

Mission patches

More than 180 embroidered patches for the nights you put in: your first card, the Moon, all twelve signs of the zodiac. A few are hidden. Some nights, something strange crosses the sky. Tap a hidden one for a hint.
