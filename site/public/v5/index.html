How to play

Point. Line it up. Collect.

Point your phone at the sky.

The app shows what's really up there, right where you're looking: satellites, planets, stars and the Moon.

Line up an object in the circle.

Hold it there and the circle locks on. Tap Collect and the card is yours.

Come back on clear nights.

Level up your cards, finish constellations star by star and earn mission patches.

Shiny cards

Catch it as it fades.

Some nights a satellite slides into Earth's shadow and vanishes mid-sky. Tap in the last seconds before it goes dark and the card comes out Shiny. Try it.
