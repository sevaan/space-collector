Space Collector iOS playtest

22 October 2026 (run overnight 21–22 Oct) · web build v0.1.1002, the same files as Build 1.0 (27) · Peterborough, Ontario (44.29, −78.33)

Summary

I played as a first-time player twice (iPhone 17, 402×874, and iPhone SE, 375×667) and as a returning player with a seeded 25-card collection (early evening, 8 PM, 9:30 PM, 1 PM, 3 PM and real time), in light and red night mode. No app console errors or warnings came up in any session. I found no stuck states, even with very fast tab switching. Red night mode tinted every screen I captured, and a pixel check found no non-red pixels. The Tonight page fix in v0.1.1002 holds: the whole page is red now.

The main problems:

  1. The weather line is wrong outside the evening. It says "Cloud now, clearing by 9:00 PM" at 1 PM and still says it at 9:30 PM.
  2. The tour card's subtitle runs together on iPhone 17: "NATURAL SATELLITEHALFWAY UP…".
  3. All around you cuts off the Sky box ("Clou…").
  4. The Testing tools' Technical block has no inner padding.

Everything else is low severity or a design suggestion.

How it was tested. The iOS Simulator control tool needed a permission grant that only you can give, so I couldn't tap in the Simulator. Instead I installed and launched the Debug build on the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 27) with simctl. Its loader and welcome card match the web build pixel for pixel (sim-02-welcome vs w02-welcome). All the interaction was in Playwright WebKit with a phone user agent, touch and safe areas, serving the project folder. I faked touch swipes with pointer and touch events and aimed by setting the drag view. Nothing was changed in the app, nothing was committed or uploaded, and the physical iPhone was not touched.

Bugs

MEDIUMTonight's weather line uses past times and says "now" for dusk

Steps
Returning player, Oct 22, forecast cloudy at dusk and clearing about 9 PM. Open the app at 1 PM, 8 PM and 9:30 PM.
Expected
At 9:30 PM: "Clear now" or "Clear until …". At 1 PM: something like "Cloudy at dusk, clearing by 9 PM".
Actual
All three say "Cloud now, clearing by 9:00 PM". That covers the TONIGHT notice in Explore (at 9:32 PM it also says "Busiest at 9:32 PM"), the Tonight's haul footer, and the All around you Sky box ("Cloud now, clearing / by 9:00 PM"). At 1 PM the "now" is false, and at 9:30 PM the "clearing by" time has already passed.
Cause
js/weather.js tonightSky() reads the hours from dusk − 30 min and builds "X now, clearing by …" from night[0], whatever time it is. The comment at main.js:2664 says judging by the evening is deliberate, but the wording needs to follow the clock once dusk has passed. Before dusk it should not say "now".

MEDIUMTour card subtitle has no gap: "NATURAL SATELLITEHALFWAY UP IN THE SOUTHWEST"

Steps
Fresh install on iPhone 17 (402 pt). Go through the tour to "Catch the Moon".
Expected
"Natural satellite · Halfway up in the southwest", or two separated parts.
Actual
The two spans touch, with no space between them. On the SE they wrap into two columns, which looks fine. Step 2 uses the same row ("Toronto sky for now / Change it in Settings") and is at risk of the same problem.
Cause
css/app.css:131 .tc .sub { display:flex; justify-content:space-between } has no gap. When the two texts fill the width there is no space left to share out.

Steps

Actual

Layout and text

  • See the three bugs above: the tour subtitle gap, the Sky box cut-off and the T5 padding.
  • The SE Shiny chances card squeezes its text into a narrow column of five lines: "CZ-8A Rocket / Stage slips into Earth's / shadow halfway / up in the northeast" (m-se5, 3rd panel).
  • The "NEXT THOR BURNER 2 ROCKET S… · 5:40 AM" chip is cut off with an ellipsis on both phones. That's acceptable, but the name is the useful part.
  • On the SE, the target ticket overlaps the circle's bottom tick (m-se3, 2nd panel).
  • The tour cards on both phones run off the bottom of the screen with no bottom border. That looks deliberate (a deck), and the buttons are always visible.
  • Strip compass letters at the far left and right of All around you are cut in half at the edges ("E", "W").
  • Settings section frames, segmented controls and the sky slider look clean. The Units/Clock AUTO buttons line up.
  • Dynamic Type: not really tested. I set the simulator to extra-extra-extra-large and relaunched, but without tap access I could only see the sealed welcome card, and card faces are fixed artwork anyway. Desktop WebKit ignores -webkit-text-size-adjust.

UX and design suggestions

  • Same-name objects. Different objects show with the same name side by side. On Tonight, the hero shows SL-14 Rocket Stage "Up now" and Coming up lists another "SL-14 Rocket Stage" at 7:45. All around you's Worth a look shows "SL-8 Rocket Stage" twice (ids 10293 and 10492). The headliner (SL-8 at 8:03) is a different SL-8 from the one New to you lists as up now. Adding the year or payload ("SL-8 · 1977") wherever a name repeats would help.
  • The first night after the tour. The Tonight page sets "Catch a satellite" as an open mission and says "It's dark: go now / clear until 2:00 AM". But satellites are in Earth's shadow until 3:19 AM (and the next good pass is 5:40 AM), after the clouds arrive. It would be fairer to say "Satellites are in Earth's shadow until 3:19 AM" there, or to swap that mission for one that can be done (w18, se10).
  • Two different counts next to each other. The radar badge says "10" (new cards up) right above "11 PASSING". New players will read these as the same thing. Maybe "10 new" in the badge, or one number only.
  • Finger mode's first view. By day the Sun sits right in the middle of the circle on launch, but the ticket says "Dark in 5 h 47 min" with no Collect until you drag (the main.js:638 gate). A finger player will think the Sun can't be caught (m-day vs d03).
  • Battery saver vs Milky Way. With Battery saver on (which means "no Milky Way"), the Milky Way switch still reads ON. You could grey it out or add "(off with Battery saver)".
  • Reminder sheet labels. The default lead is tagged "· BEST". After picking 5 min in Settings, 5 min is tagged "· USUAL" and 10 min loses "BEST". "Your default" in both cases would be clearer (s13).
  • Settings icons. Battery saver uses the speaker icon (the same as Sounds). The sparkle icon is used for six rows (Plus, Stars, Milky Way, Satellites by day, Meteor showers, How dark). "RESET TO 0°" shows under Compass nudge even when it's already 0°.
  • Repeated words in the Tonight hero. By day the hero header reads "TONIGHT'S HEADLINER · +25 XP · CLOUD FORECAST", and the line under the art repeats "UNCOMMON · NEW TO YOU · HEADLINER" (m-dt).
  • "Over Africa · not visible tonight" appears for a CZ-3 stage drawn in your daytime sky with Satellites by day on. For high-orbit objects this reads as a contradiction (ds01).
  • Welcome card. After the flip there's "↑ SWIPE AWAY" above the card and "↑ SWIPE UP TO BEGIN" inside it. One is enough.
  • Two forecast notices at once. By day the Sun warning and the Tonight weather notice stack and push the radar to the middle of the screen (m-day).

What works well

  • First run. Loader → sealed card → flip → swipe up → three tour cards → "Catch the Moon" → Show me → Collect → heartbeat-free Common reveal (+110 XP, Orionids 2026 and First Light chips, phase stamp 1/8) → Tonight page with the missions ticked → the First Light patch coin in Explore. The decline path (Not now / Use a finger / Not now) goes through the same flow with "Toronto sky for now". Replay the tour returns to the sealed card.
  • Returning player. The Your Week recap is lovely. Target states read clearly: an orange Collect for new, a cream VIEW CARD for owned, NEW STAMP on the Moon, "Below the horizon · not collected yet · not in your sky for 12+ hours", HIDDEN BY DAYLIGHT, and the coral JUST A PLANE with route, type, height and range.
  • "Show objects that aren't visible". Draws the secrets as named dashed rings ("Hello from Earth", "The Golden Record", "Wow!").
  • Tonight page. Hero, Coming up, New to you, Seen before (opens the card), haul with the rarity chart, headliner, Tonight's run with Shiny chance and weather rows, Shiny chances, the PLUS teaser, missions, the Every pass chart and the next clear night. Tapping a jump chip scrolls and lights the right section. The ISS bell is free and the others are dimmed, as designed. The reminder sheet works and closes with a pull down. The whole page also closes with a pull down from the content.
  • All around you. Landscape by day and night, card art on the strip, "FACING S · TAP TO COME BACK" after a drag. Worth a look cards kept their order: the same order on open, after 6 s and after a drag. Tapping a card closes the page and aims (the Moon came up with Collect ready).
  • Collection. All four tabs. The card viewer double-tap flip shows the album back and swipe goes to the next card (1/25 → 2/25). Press and hold peek lifts the card over a blurred grid, and letting go doesn't open the viewer. Observer stats opened from Settings goes back to Settings, and Settings goes back to Explore.
  • Settings. Every segmented control takes effect. Clock 24 h changes the Tonight header (20:11), all list times and the reminder sheet (05:05). Units MI changes the satellite ticket (705 mi). Reminders lead moves the sheet's tag. Milky Way off and Battery saver remove the Milky Way. Random card back is locked and explains how to unlock it. The How to play deck has 7 cards with dots that follow the scroll position and ends on See tonight, which opens Tonight. Plus sheet prices are right (Year saves 37% against monthly). Time travel ±1 h and Jump to next visible pass ("JUMPED AHEAD 2:58 AM · Breeze-M Debris is up in the N") both work.
  • Page switching. I rapid-tapped the tabs about 30 times at 120 ms, again at 40 ms, then ended on each tab in turn. Each time the right page and header settled, with nothing left on top. Settings opened from Tonight goes back to Tonight. On All around you the corner button is ✕ and closes the page, as designed.
  • Console. No app errors or warnings in any session. The only messages were Playwright's "Service Worker registration blocked" and one NotFoundError from my own synthetic setPointerCapture (test harness, not the app).
