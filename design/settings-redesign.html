C · Star chart

Open rows on a faint chart grid, dashed section rules, and switches that are the app's target circle: an empty ring when off, locked on in orange when on. The slider's thumb is the reticle too.

Same in all three

  • Every row, setting and behaviour stays the same; only the look changes.
  • No coloured icon tiles: cream line icons that take red night mode for free.
  • Fonts are the card fonts (Barlow Condensed, Russo One).

Trade-offs

  • A ties Settings to the cards most strongly; it's the busiest.
  • B has the most character; rocker switches need a bit more care for touch size.
  • C is the calmest and matches the sky screen; least decoration.
My pick: A. Settings is where people go between catches, and framing each group as a card from the collection makes it feel like part of the game rather than a phone menu. The ON/OFF tags read clearly in red night mode too. C is a close second if you'd like it quieter.
