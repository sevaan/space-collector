Target ticket + mini card

Today's Explore screen and today's ticket under the circle, now carrying the real mini card from your Collection. These are screenshots of the actual tiles: cards you don't own show the hidden picture, cards you own show everything, foil and stamps included.

How it would work: the mini card is the same one the Collection draws, so there's nothing new to keep in sync. Tapping the card (not just the button) opens it. In Option 2 the slider steps aside while the card stands up, as it does today when a ticket reaches it, and the card sinks back as the circle lets go. Room's take: Option 1 for new things (the hidden card plus Collect is a clear "this could be yours"); Option 2 for things you own, because seeing your actual card, with its level and foil, is the reward for coming back to it.
