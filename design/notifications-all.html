Notifications: every type

Every notification the sky can show, in the top right under the Headliner chip. Left: today, captured from the app on an iPhone 15. Right: the redesign. Same width, place and colours; one layout for all of them.

One layout. An icon tile, a short kind line, a bold title, and an optional detail line. Everything has a kind line now, so a bare sentence never floats on its own.
One thing on the right. XP for rewards, › when tapping goes somewhere, a light ✕ for notices that stay. No big yellow buttons, no boxed ✕.
Pictures as icons. The compass and constellation pictures shrink to the 32-pt tile, so the words get the full width.
Four colours. Orange rewards (missions, rank, card backs), gold the night's news (events, milestones, Tonight), steel blue progress (stamps, constellations, reminders), cream info; red only for a failure; the shiny one gets the shiny edge.
Two lines each, at most. Titles say the thing ("Navigator", "Sky over Toronto"); the detail says what to do or why. Words rewritten to fit.
Tap anywhere. The whole notice is the button; the › or the "See ›" in the detail just says so.
