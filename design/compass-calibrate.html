Line up the sky

Replace the ±5° compass nudge with one button in Settings that takes you to Explore in a lining-up mode, then back to the normal sky. The app picks something real to line up against (the Moon, else the brightest planet, else a bright star), so you don't have to guess.

FallbackNo Moon, planet or bright star up (daytime, cloud): line up against a compass direction instead. The Sun is never offered.
  • Picking the reference. The Moon if it's up and more than 10° high, else the brightest planet (Venus, Jupiter, Mars, Saturn), else the brightest named star, else a compass direction. "Drag instead" is always there.
  • Figure-8 first. If the phone reports a poor compass (the existing compass-doubt check), the mode opens with "Wave your phone in a slow figure-8" for a few seconds before step 1, since lining up a confused compass won't last.
  • Drifting phones. On a phone with no compass (Android browsers that only give the gyro, "relative"), lining up is the only fix, so after a few minutes of use the app can offer it: a "Sky drifting? Line it up" notice that opens this mode.
  • What it changes. One saved offset (the existing compass nudge, already kept across restarts and locked against small compass wobbles). This replaces the ±5° steppers; the camera-view drag line-up stays as it is.
  • My pick: A (aim, then confirm) as the main path, with B (drag) one tap away, and the north fallback.
