Free and Plus

Space Collector · plans to work through · 2026-10-11

The rule I've used throughout: the sky is never behind a paywall. Anyone can point at anything, collect every card, earn every patch and play every mission. Plus pays for the things that cost us money to run, the things that help you plan, and the things that look nice.

Decided so far: one plan, Space Collector Plus, at about $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year, or $39.99 for life. Cloud backup and sync stay free. Pass alerts, planning ahead, binocular mode and cosmetics are Plus. All the new Plus ideas below are in. When the native apps ship, the web version comes down (kept only as Sevaan's testing build), so Plus is sold only through Apple and Google.

Daily limits, like "10 catches a day free, 20 on Plus"

I'd avoid limiting catches. In most collecting games a limit just means "come back tomorrow". Here, the thing you'd be limited on is a real pass that happens once, right now, with you standing outside. Hitting "you've used your 10 catches" while the ISS crosses overhead is the worst moment the app could give someone. It would also leave the 11th catch with nowhere to go, since it can't be saved and caught later.

If you want a limit, put it on something that isn't tied to a moment in the sky:

LIMIT

Reminders and alerts per night

Free gets 1 reminder a night, Plus is unlimited. You still see everything; you just get fewer nudges.

LIMIT

How far ahead you can plan

Free sees tonight, Plus sees the week. Already decided, and it's the natural limit.

LIMIT

Mission rerolls

Swap a mission you can't do tonight (cloud, wrong part of the sky): free 1 a week, Plus 1 a night.

AVOID

Catch or card limits, energy, timers

Anything that stops you collecting what's in front of you.

AVOID

Paid XP boosts, paid shinies, card packs or loot boxes

They undercut the honesty of "you really saw this".

AVOID

Ads

They'd break the quiet night-sky feel, and bright ad screens ruin night vision.

PERK

For anyone who buys Plus in the first months: a thank-you that costs nothing to make. It's a patch, not a power.

7 days of Plus. Possibly also free Plus during big meteor-shower peaks, so everyone gets a taste.

PERK

Apple and Google both support sharing a subscription. Good for parents stargazing with kids.

How it would work underneath

  1. RevenueCat tracks who has Plus across the web, iPhone and Android, keyed to the Firebase account you already have. One switch in the app (isPlus) turns perks on.
  2. On iPhone and Android the purchase has to go through Apple's and Google's in-app purchases (they take 15% for small developers).
  3. The web version becomes a private testing build once the apps ship, so there's no web checkout.
  4. The pass-alert server checks Plus before sending.
  5. Accounts: buying Plus needs sign-in, so it follows you to a new phone.
