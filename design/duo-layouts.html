iPhone Duo: the unfolded layouts

Three different ways to use the unfolded screen for each of Tonight, the Collection and Settings. Every mock-up is the Duo's inner screen on its side (951 × 669 pt, drawn at 60%), with the status bar strip on the right and the fold marked faintly down the middle. The pick for each also shows turned upright (669 × 951). Built from the app's real art and real section names. The question running through all of them: split the content across the fold (a book: one thing per side), or keep it off the fold.
