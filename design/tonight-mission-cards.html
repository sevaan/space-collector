For every option

  • Where: right under the sky window, above Tonight’s chase. The missions list lower on the page goes; the week’s theme and the night’s news stay down there.
  • The picture is the card of the pass that finishes the mission (the Ariane 5 stage, Kosmos 1589). Missions about stars or planets show the thing that’s up; done ones show what you caught.
  • A tap aims at it (Show me for a pass, Aim for a star or planet), the same as the chase cards.
  • Finishing one during the night: the card flips to its COMPLETE side with the +50, like the reveal.
