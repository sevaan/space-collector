The new-card screen: four directions (card at today's size, no scrolling)

Same card (SPOT 5, Uncommon, +20 XP) in each. All four add a share button and animate the points: the +20 pops in, counts up, and sparks fly into your rank bar or XP total, which fills. Everything loops here every few seconds so you can watch it; in the app it plays once. A is the most theatrical, D is the smallest change from today. Each phone is the real space in Safari on your iPhone (390 × 700 pt) with the card at today's size (339 pt wide), so everything here fits without scrolling.
