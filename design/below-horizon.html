Below the horizon: the recommended set, by tilt

Each tilt angle has one job. Near the horizon (frames 1–2): solid ground with your tree line, and a departures lane of what's rising soon, each at the direction it will come up, with a countdown. Looking straight down (frame 3): the compass at your feet and a short "what's next" list with arrows. Frame 4 is the alternative X-ray take (see-through Earth) for comparison. The circle dims and sky labels hide whenever you point below the horizon.
