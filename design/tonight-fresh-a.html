What’s still wrong with “A and B together”

  • It’s a weather report with cards attached. Verdict, window, picks, list: it tells me about the sky but never gives me something to finish tonight. The rewards are 12px pills at the end of rows.
  • It assumes a clear evening. Cloud got one sentence: “same layout, greyed”. On the most common Ontario night the page becomes three grey rows and no reason to come back.
  • “Clear” and “empty” are the same colour. It has no idea of Earth’s shadow. Tonight’s only clear hours (12–2 AM) have zero passes, so a clear-night layout would point people at a dead sky.
  • Four systems, stapled. Three picks, three missions, a headliner and a week strip are different threes. The player has to do the joining.
  • The live card copies Explore. A countdown plus “turn left, then up” is the sky screen’s job. This page should send you out, not be a second sky.
  • The hook is buried. “Tomorrow” and “what you missed” only appear after 10:40 PM, when most people have closed the app. “42 more new cards · All passes” trains list-scrolling instead of focus.

The concept: Tonight’s hand

Every night the game deals you three sealed cards, face down in your own card back. Each one is a real object you can actually catch tonight, picked with the forecast and Earth’s shadow already worked in, and each carries one of the night’s three missions. That gives the page one job: flip all three. A full hand pays a bonus. Under the hand, the night dial draws dusk to dawn as an arc: cloud on the outside, how busy the sky is in the middle, Earth’s shadow on the inside, your cards pinned where they happen. A night the cloud ruins isn’t wasted. Any dealt card the cloud hides gets a Rain check stamp and moves to your next clear night with its bonus kept. And when the sky is clear but every satellite is in Earth’s shadow, the deal switches to Night shift cards (the Moon, planets, stars), which don’t need sunlight. The other 718 passes are one tap away, never on the page.

How the deal is pickedOnly objects you can really see: in a clear forecast hour, sunlit, bright enough for your sky slider. One card per mission. At least one is new to you, rarest first.
