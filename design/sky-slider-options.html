The sky slider on Explore

Today it's a full-width slider above the tabs (SEE LESS ↔ SEE MORE) that dims when you're not using it. Four other ways to do the same job: tell the app how dark your sky is (and, past the end, binoculars). Each mock-up plays on a loop.

My pick: B. Most of the time you don't touch the slider, so it shouldn't take a whole row of the screen. A small pill that names your sky keeps the setting visible, and one tap opens the full slider when you want it. D is the easiest to understand (five named skies, no fiddly dragging) but loses fine control. C frees the bottom completely and is a thumb's reach away, but it's less discoverable. E is the most fun: you literally see stars appear as you slide, but it's the biggest piece of chrome.
