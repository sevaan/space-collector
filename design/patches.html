Achievements as mission patches

Real missions each get an embroidered patch; here every achievement does too. Patches live on their own wall (not in the card count), a few unlock card backs, and earning one gets a short stitched-on moment. Three styles to choose from first, then the wall, the detail view, the earning moment and the card-back rewards, all drawn in the first style.

A · Embroidered roundel

The classic NASA patch: a round twill badge with a raised stitched border, the name curving across the top and the category along the bottom, one bold icon in the middle. Each family has its own twill colour (collection navy, rarity purple, rockets rust, nations blue, history sepia, fleets teal, planets amber, constellations indigo, sky skills green, time maroon).

B · Shapes by family

Same stitching and colours, but each family gets its own silhouette: shields for rarity, triangles for rockets, hexagons for nations, ovals for planets, stars for constellations. The wall reads at a glance by shape.

C · Enamel pins

Hard enamel: flat colour, a polished gold rim and a glossy highlight that catches the tilt like the shiny cards. Crisper and more "collectible object" than fabric, less of a mission feel.

D · Foil patches (the card finishes)

The rare and big patches get the same finishes as the cards: gold foil (the completed-constellation gold) for milestone patches, and holo (the shiny-card rainbow, gentle, moving with the phone's tilt) for the rarest. Hover/tilt the board to see the sheen move. Plain patches stay plain, so a foil one means something.

The patch wall, the detail view

Left: Collection gets a fourth tab, Patches. Family chips filter; earned patches are full colour, unearned ones are a faint stitch outline with the name (so you can see what's out there). Right: tap a patch for the detail: the requirement, when you earned it, and a reward if it has one.

Patch wall

3 across, like a jacket covered in patches. 18 of 75 earned shown.

Patch detail

Big patch that tilts with the phone (the card tilt), what it's for, the date, and the card back it unlocked.

Earning one

Shorter than a card reveal: the patch drops in, the border stitches itself around (a dashed line drawing on), it settles with a small thump, then shrinks into a toast that stays the usual few seconds.

Card-back rewards

A handful of big patches unlock a new back for all your cards (set in Settings; the card back system already exists). Rewards that change your cards rather than more cards.
