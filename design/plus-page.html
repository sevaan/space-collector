C · Show the extras

One honest line that the game stays free, then each extra shown as the real thing: a widget, a notification, the shiny window, the card backs fanned out, the patch.

+ People see what they get instead of reading a list; best for converting.

− Less of your "support the app" voice; the most to build and keep up to date.

My pick: A's words with C's pictures. Lead with "The whole sky is free." and the free-always list (A), then show each extra as the real thing (C) instead of a list, and end on "Support Space Collector". One App Store note: because Plus unlocks features, it has to stay a normal subscription/purchase. Calling it "support" is fine, but the button can't say "donate".
