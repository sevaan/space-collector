Your sky, as a tour card

Step 2 of the tour, teaching the sky slider instead of asking inside or outside. Four ways, each by day and at night. The sliders work: drag them. By day there are no real stars to match, so each design says so and lets you set it roughly now (the "Match your sky" card still comes once after dark).

ATry it in the card

A little sky window right in the card with its own slider. Drag and the stars come and go, the city glow fades, the readout says "Suburbs · about 550 stars". You learn it by doing it, and it sets the real slider.

BPick the sky like yours

Four small sky pictures, from a bright city to a dark site; tap the one like where you are. It sets the slider for you, and the card says where the slider lives to fine-tune it later. Fastest; works by day because you know where you live.

CPoint at the real slider

The card shrinks and points down at the actual slider, which pulses. Drag it and the real sky behind fills with stars. Teaches the exact control you'll use; best at night (by day the sky stays blue, so it says to come back after dark).

DWatch it work

A short loop: a finger slides the knob and the stars in the window fill in, then fade back. One button, "Got it". Quickest to read; nothing to do.
