Now, Tonight and Week, through users' eyes

Seven people of different ages used the three "Up in your sky" tabs over a few evenings. Below is what they said, then ideas, then three directions to redesign around. Nothing is built yet.

Now

What they want: something to do right now, and where to point.

"The map is pretty but I don't know what the dots are. I just want to know which one to look at."

Ideas

Everything worth considering, roughly from most to least wanted. Tags mark what could be Plus.

  1. Next thing, with a countdown

    Now opens on the next card worth getting: big name, rarity, "in 4:32", an arrow that points the way as you turn. Taps through to aim.

  2. Tonight's run

    An itinerary of 3 to 6 stops (dark, best catches, shiny chance, busiest moment, cloud arriving), each saying what it gets you: a new Epic, finishes "Catch something 60° up", +40 XP.

  3. Tonight's haul forecast

    A small summary: "Go out 8 to 9 and you could get about 6 new cards, including 1 Epic, and finish 2 missions." The reason to go outside.

  4. Remind me for my runPlus for more than one

    One tap sets reminders for every stop. Free players pick their one stop.

  5. Chase list

    Star cards you want. Tonight and Week flag when they pass; a pass alert fires for them first. Gives the collection a goal.

  6. Week as a 7-day strip

    One row of days: weather icon, Moon phase, a dot when a rare card is only up that night. Tap a day for its run.

  7. "Only this week" moments

    Rare windows: an Epic that won't pass again for weeks, a shiny eclipse night, a meteor shower peak, a satellite about to re-enter. Urgency that's true.

  8. Plain-language directions

    "Low in the southeast, start above the trees" and "Rises behind you" instead of degrees. Optional landmark labels later.

  9. Commons collapsed

    Lists lead with Uncommon and rarer; "+212 Commons" opens the rest.

  10. Family plan

    Share tonight's run as a message: "Stargazing at 8:30? Epic satellite at 8:39."

  11. Weekly goal ring

    "3 of 5 new cards this week" on the Week tab, tied to the streak.

Three directions

Each one reshapes all three tabs around a single idea. They can be mixed.

An itinerary for the night

  • Now: the next stop of tonight's run, live countdown, arrow.
  • Tonight: 3 to 6 stops on a short timeline with the haul forecast at the top and "Remind me for my run".
  • Week: each day shows the size of its run (3 stops, 1 Epic).
  • Best for Priya, Leo and Kofi. Most "game-like" reason to go out.
