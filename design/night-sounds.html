Night sounds

Real recordings for the proposed night ambience: a very quiet bed under “Permafrost” that follows the time and the weather (idea 05 on The catch, the feel). Every file is CC0 (public domain): no credit needed, can't be withdrawn.

How to use it: ▶ plays a candidate on its own, loud enough to judge. Turn on Play under the music to hear it at an in-game level (music at 50% × 0.15, the night bed well under it). Pick one per slot, then play a whole night in the Mix. Each clip is a 32 s seamless loop (the end is crossfaded into the start), all levelled to −30 LUFS so they compare fairly. Your picks are saved in this browser.

Night bed

Mix · a whole night in a minute

Pick one per slot and hear the night play through: dusk (crickets and wind), midnight (the owl, the crickets slowing, the wind), dawn (first birds). In the game each change crossfades over 20 s; here each part lasts 20 s so you hear the shape quickly. The optional still-night and snow layers join only once you've picked one (the snow mix uses snow 1 until you do).

Notes. The descriptions under each clip come from the recordist's own notes and a level scan of the file (where the louder moments fall), not from listening, so trust your ears over them. Clips are cut from Freesound's 128 kbps preview MP3s (the full-quality originals need a free login); for the game we'd fetch the originals and re-cut the chosen loops. Originals, titles, authors, links and licence for every file are in night-sounds/sources.json; the untouched downloads are in night-sounds/raw/.
