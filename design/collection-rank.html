Collection: room for the cards

On a small phone (375 × 667, shown here) the rank card fills the first screen of Mine, so you see no cards until you scroll. Four ways to fix it. Each mock-up is that phone’s first screen.

My pick: A. You keep what’s useful at a glance (your rank, how close the next one is, Missions) in one row, and two full rows of cards show on the first screen. The ladder picture moves into Observer stats, which a tap on the row opens. D keeps the picture but moves while you scroll, which is more to get used to; C is the cleanest but hides Missions one tap further away.
