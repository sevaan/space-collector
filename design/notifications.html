The rules

Look up

  • When: the start of the evening's best window: dark enough, and the busiest stretch before 11 pm. Never later than 10:30 pm.
  • Weather: only if the forecast is clear for that hour. Checked again just before it fires; cloudy, rain or fog means no notification.
  • Once a day, and not if you've already opened the app that evening.

Catch of the day

  • Tonight's headliner, 10 minutes before it rises. Free, and doesn't use the one free reminder.
  • Skipped if the weather's bad at that time, or you already have the card.
  • On a cloudy night neither one comes: the next clear night gets them instead.

Everything else

  • Only what you ask for: reminders from a bell (passes, "remind me at dark", the week's best night).
  • The automatic Plus pass alerts (up to 3 a night) go away.
  • Settings → Notifications has two switches: Look up, and Catch of the day.
One thing to decide: automatic pass alerts are a Plus perk today. Under these rules they'd be dropped, so Plus would lose that perk. Unlimited reminders stay as the Plus notification perk.
