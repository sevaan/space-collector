Space Collector · Plus cosmetics · 16 Oct 2026

Card backs

Six new backs in the same family as today's: the same wordmark, frame line and screen-print style, each with its own theme. Move your pointer or finger over a card to tilt it.

How they'd work

Choosing

  • Settings → Card back: a row of backs; tap one and every card in your collection, the reveal and shares use it.
  • Mission Control stays the default and free for everyone.

Plus, and a few to earn

  • Plus unlocks them all.
  • One or two could also be earned free, so they double as rewards: Golden Record for finding the Pale Blue Dot story, Star Chart for completing 10 constellations.

Shows off

  • The back shows mid-flip in the reveal, on every double-tap flip, and on shared cards, so friends see it.
  • Nebula Foil gets the holo shimmer as you tilt.
To decide: which backs to keep or redo, and whether any are earned as rewards instead of Plus-only. My pick for launch: all six for Plus, with Golden Record and Star Chart also earnable.
