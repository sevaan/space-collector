Card writing sample

31 satellite cards. On each: the line under the name now says what it does, and the fact is rewritten to read smoothly. Every number and claim is from the existing sourced fact unless noted (DFH 1’s song, TIROS 1’s 78 days, Landsat’s 16-day repeat, the ISS crew since 2000, Hubble and the age of the universe, Aqua’s water focus, Radarsat seeing through cloud). Nothing is live yet.

Vanguard 1

Now

American satellite · 1958

The oldest human-made object still in orbit. Launched in 1958, it was the first satellite with solar power. Its radio fell silent in 1964.

Proposed

American test satellite · 1958

The oldest human-made object still in orbit, up since 1958. It was the first satellite to run on solar power; its radio went quiet in 1964.
