Fragment cards: two ideas

A: the parent object intact but smaller, pieces of it flying off. B: the parent dim behind, one piece of it sharp in front. Same parent picture as each card's object, tinted like other shared pictures.

One real photo (or the builder's own official illustration) per satellite design, used for every card built on it. Today's picture beside it. Credits say Photo or Illustration.

Same accurate shapes (each card's own size and shape from GCAT, J. McDowell), three looks: today's picture, a lit scene like the painted cards, and an engraved scene like the restyled cards. Body is bare metal and cells are blue: the data doesn't say gold foil, so none is added.

For satellites with no free photo: each card drawn to scale from its own catalogue entry (shape, length, diameter, span, from GCAT by J. McDowell). Nothing added that the catalogue doesn't say. Debris gets broken fragments instead of a whole rocket stage. The small picture under each card is what it shows today.
