When to show Plus

Today Plus only appears when someone hits a perk (a grey bell, a locked back, “+N more” shiny chances, the widget). These are the moments where it would offer itself, captured from the real app with the new lines dropped in. Nothing here is built yet.

1 · The invite

A good night ends (5+ catches, or a new Rare or better, put away), but only from your 4th night on, never your first three; or it’s your 7th night, by day. At most once every 14 days, three times ever. Two ways to show it:

A · A notice firstDrops in from the top after the card is put away. Tap it and the Join the crew page opens. Swipe it away and that’s that. Gentle; my pick.
→
The page, with its reasonSame Join the crew page, the gold pill says why it opened: “Five catches tonight. Nights like this are what Plus is for.” Option B opens this straight away, no notice.
7th night“Seven nights under the sky. You’re one of us now.” Shown by day, the next calm moment after your 7th night.

2 · Lines where a perk would help

One line each, never a pop-up. They only open the page if tapped.

Tonight · Rares comingOn a clear night with 2+ Rares still to come, the generic “Set reminders… with Plus” line becomes “3 Rares tonight · get a ping before each one ›”.
Earning a card backNext to the “New card back” chip: “1 of 7 · see them all ›”. (Captured on a card view; it would show on the catch that earns it.)
Weekly recapUnder its buttons: “Put next week on your home screen · Plus ›”, opening the page on the Widgets tile.

3 · Sharing the moments with reviews

Good idea. People rate an app when they’re proud of something; they join when they want more of what just happened. So split the moments by feeling, and never ask for both on the same day.

  • One ask a day at most, review or Plus. Reviews keep their 60-day gap, the Plus invite its 14-day gap.
  • Never Plus on the first 3 nights, after a miss, on a cloudy night, or where it can’t be bought (Android for now).
  • Same calm rules as reviews: nothing in the circle, no page, card, tour or Collection up, not in red mode, no new pass within 20 minutes.
  • Three closed invites and it stops; the lines in section 2 stay, they’re only ever one line.
My pick: invite A (notice first) after a great night and on the 7th night; the Tonight line and the card-back chip; the recap line only on weeks the recap isn’t asking for a review. The split in section 3 for everything else.
