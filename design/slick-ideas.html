Slicker, screen by screen

Sixteen small things that would make Space Collector feel more expensive. Nothing here changes the game. It's all about how it moves, sounds and feels in the hand. Each idea has a small moving mock-up so you can see the motion.

How to read the mock-ups: the little ◉ tags light up orange at the exact moment the phone would buzz, and the ♪ tags when a sound would play. Effort: small is an afternoon of CSS or a few lines of code, medium is a day or two, large is more. Everything would turn off under Reduce Motion, and the buzzes follow the Vibration switch. I left out things you've already turned down (drag-to-throw, stamps, practice mode, the meteor chip, the radar tip) and things that already exist (the sky swinging into place in the tour, the lit chip on the sky page, the tilting patch detail, Show me's flying target).

My top five

If I could only do five, in this order. Three of them are small.

  1. 02 · Warmer, warmer… got itIt's the core of the game. Pointing is the thing you do most, and right now you can't feel a target coming. This makes the hunt itself feel good.
  2. 07 · It goes into your binderEvery catch ends here. Seeing the card drop into the Collection tab and the count go up is the "it's mine now" moment. Matches the patch coins.
  3. 05 · A heartbeat before the big onesSmall to build, and it's what people will tell friends about. It only fires on Epic and Legendary, so it stays rare.
  4. 06 · The numbers count upAn afternoon of work that makes every single card (not just rare ones) feel more alive when it lands.
  5. 14 · Lights outTiny, fun, and it fits the night theme perfectly. A good thing to show in the App Store preview.

Notes: haptics on the website fall back to nothing, which is fine. Rapid ticks (03, 09) should stay light and be capped so they never buzz more than about ten times a second. For 03, the leaning ring should be tiny (a few pixels) so it never makes aiming harder. Mock-ups are rough; real versions would use the app's own reticle, cards and sounds.
