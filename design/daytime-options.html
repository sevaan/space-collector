Daytime Explore: the sky as it is, plus tonight

By day, Explore shows the real daytime sky (pale blue, the Sun with a don't-look guard, your horizon) with dashed "ghost" outlines of what's up there but can't be seen until dark: stars, constellation lines and satellites with where they are right now. A Tonight briefing card sits above the switcher: when it gets dark, the best pass you don't own (Remind me), the Moon, a mission, and the cloud forecast for tonight. The sky also takes on your local weather (Open-Meteo, free, no key): clouds drift, rain falls, snow falls, fog rolls. When the Moon is up by day it's called out loudly, because you can catch it right now. Animated: open this page to see the weather move.
