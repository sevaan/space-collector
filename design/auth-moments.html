Signing in: when and where

No sign-in step in the tour. The app asks at three moments when there's something worth keeping, and every sign-in is one tap, with no passwords. The first time you use Apple or Google, the account is created, so there's no separate "create account" step. Sign in with Apple goes first on iPhone, because Apple requires it alongside Google. Every option has "Not now" except buying Plus.
