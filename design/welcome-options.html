Welcome card: four directions that send you outside

The card you see after tapping the sealed card on first launch. All four are built with the real card renderer (same frame as every card in the app) and keep "Swipe up to begin" at the foot. Each says the same three things in a different way: go outside, look up, point your phone at a light and collect it. The art is drawn for the card; it can be swapped for a painted picture later.
