The figure comes into focus

Orion, drawn with the game's real card code. Each star card of a constellation shows the figure behind the stick lines, stronger with every star of it you own. Left to right: 1, 7, 15 and all 23 of Orion's stars.

A · Fades in

The figure is a faint ghost at first and gets more solid with each star.

B · Comes into focus

The figure starts blurred and faint and sharpens as you go, like focusing a telescope. At all 23 it's crisp, with "Orion complete".

Things to decide

  • Art: 18 constellations already have figure pictures (the zodiac, Orion, Big Dipper, Little Dipper, Cassiopeia, Cygnus, Southern Cross). The other 70 need one each; I'd generate them in the same style.
  • Big ones: Orion has 23 stars, so one star barely changes it. Progress is by share of stars, so small constellations (Scutum has 4) jump a lot per card.
  • Where it shows: on every star card of that constellation (they all share one progress), and on the constellation's patch.
My pick: B, coming into focus. It reads as discovering the picture, not just a brightness change, and the blur hides the figure's details until you've earned them.
