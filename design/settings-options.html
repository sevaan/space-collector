Settings: four layouts

Every option holds the same settings: today's plus the new ones (NEW): a Notifications group with meteor showers and a default reminder time, Milky Way, planes, sky labels, red mode after dark, units, clock, battery saver, and Help (how to play, feedback, rate). Export is gone. Scroll inside each phone.

My pick: B. The list is long now (about 35 rows), and B reads fastest: the phone's own Settings layout, so nobody has to learn it, with our colours and type. A keeps today's field-card look but gets heavy at this length. C makes the first screen short and friendly, at the cost of one more tap for everything. D puts the four things people change at night (red mode, sound, music, labels) as big buttons at the top, with the sky slider as a gauge, and is the most "in the game".
