Notices under the header

Today a notice drops over the top of the screen and covers the header (the moon and Settings buttons) for a few seconds. Four ways to keep the header free: the notice always appears below the double line. Each mock-up plays on a loop; the header always stays on top and tappable.

My pick: A. It reads like a real notification, keeps all the information we show today (kind, title, detail, XP), and it comes from somewhere: out from under the header. The radar and chip step down while it's there, so nothing is covered, and they slide back up after. B is the calmest (one line) but has to drop the detail line; C looks the most built-in; D keeps the sky clearest but is cramped on small phones.
