One sky page

Now and Tonight merged into one page, opened from the radar. It answers, in order: what can I catch right this minute, what's next and when, what does the rest of the night look like, and what am I working towards (missions). Example: 8:30 PM, clear, dark from 6:50 PM.

What a player is asking when they open it: "Is anything good up right now?", then "When's the next good one, and where?", then "Is tonight worth staying out?", and "What should I go for?" Today those answers are split across two tabs, so the next pass and the plan never sit together. A answers them top to bottom in one scroll and is the closest to today. B is the most visual and makes "later" feel explorable, but the scrubbing has to be discovered. C is the calmest and most glanceable, and ties missions to actual passes ("counts for a planet or the Moon"), which gives people a reason to catch them. My pick: C, with A's timeline behind "Everything tonight".
