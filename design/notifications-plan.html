Device notifications: the plan

Two new rooms looked at this: three UX designers (games and retention, calm utility apps, astronomy apps) and two UX writers (games, calm utility). Below is their work combined, with my calls where they clash with things you've already decided. Their verdict: the app's taste in what to send is good; how it sends is weak. Everything is worked out on the phone while the app is open, so alerts can't check the weather when they fire, someone who hasn't opened the app for a few days gets nothing, and tapping a notification doesn't take you anywhere.

Fix how it sends

  • Tap goes somewhere: straight to the sky aimed at the thing, or to the Tonight page.
  • Two buttons: Show me · Not tonight (silences the rest of tonight's automatic ones, instead of turning everything off).
  • Android arrives late: its delivery window runs to 4 min after the time, so "5 minutes before" can land after rising. End the window at the time instead.

Send more, where it's worth it

  • Come back: someone away for 3, 7 and 21 days gets one notification each, naming a real pass, then silence.
  • Tell me if it breaks becomes a real notification.
  • Meteor shower peak night, eclipses, last chance, planet at its best.

Keep it from being muted

  • A budget per evening by kind, not one daily number. What you asked for is never limited.
  • Look up only on nights that are worth it, at most 4 a week.
  • If 5 in a row are ignored, the nightly nudges drop to one a week until you open the app.

How often

Per evening

  • Asked for: unlimited.
  • Automatic: one from Tonight, one sky event, and for Plus up to two sky alerts. So free tops out at 2, Plus at 4, on a big night.
  • Two within 20 minutes merge into one ("…then a Rare at 7:31").

Per week

  • Look up and streak: 4 a week at most.
  • Constellations, planets, 7-day shower notices: 2 a week.
  • Expected: a free player in a clear week gets 3–5, a Plus player 6–9, someone away gets 3 over three weeks, then nothing.

Quiet and brakes

  • Nothing automatic 11 PM–7 AM; with Night owl, 2–7 AM.
  • 5 ignored in a row → nightly nudges drop to 1 a week until you open the app.
  • Never notify on unknown weather. Never the same title two days running.

Reminders you set are the bells; they don't need a switch. Android gets four channels with the same names, so its own settings screen agrees.

Asking for permission

  • Keep the tour's step 3 ("Know when to look up"). You put it back on 18 October. The designers wanted to move the system prompt to the first-night appointment, but you've already decided this, so I'd keep it in the tour.
  • When someone taps Not now on iPhone, ask for quiet delivery (provisional). Notifications then land silently in Notification Center with Apple's own Keep / Turn off. The first one should be a daytime "Tonight: Tiangong at 7:20 PM", since a quiet 5-minute alert is useless.
  • The first-night appointment ("Tell me at 7:42 PM") becomes the second chance to ask.
  • Android allows two asks before "don't ask again": the tour, then a bell you tap later. After that, the bell opens the phone's settings.
  • When it's off: the bells say "Notifications are off" on the button, and Settings shows the banner. Never ask again by ourselves.

Tour wording

Plus

Free

Everything that's part of the game or comes back once a year: the headliner, almost there, tell me if it breaks, look up, coming back, streak, every sky event including Halloween, and reminders for the Space Station, dark, a clear night and a shower's peak.

Moving sky events back to free follows your rule ("everything playable is free"): showers and Halloween earn badges and patches, and they're the notifications that make people keep you allowed.

Plus

Reminders for any pass (the bell) and Sky alerts (rare passes, Starlink trains, last chance, with the shiny chance mentioned when there is one). Convenience, not content: the passes are all on the Tonight page anyway.

  • Turn Sky alerts on when Plus starts. Today Rare alerts start off, so a trial can go 7 days without hearing one.
  • The Plus page names the thing you tapped: "Reminders for Hubble are part of the crew kit."
  • A "your trial ends in 2 days" notification (fewer refunds and angry reviews).
  • I'd skip the designers' weekly "Rares passed while you were inside" line for free players. You took Plus nudges off Explore on 27 October, and this is the same thing.

The words

Voice rules (from the writers)

  • Time first, then a middle dot: "7:42 PM · The Space Station". A colon reads "19:42: Tiangong" on a 24-hour clock.
  • "Now" only when it stays true: "Now · It’s dark", never "Now · 14 satellites up" (it's read late).
  • One path sentence everywhere: "Comes up in the west, climbs high in the south."
  • Use the real tier: "A new Legendary", not "a rare find".
  • Honest about weather: "should", "about", "says the forecast".
  • No guilt, no "we miss you", no exclamation marks, no emoji, never the app's name.

Wording bugs to fix now

  • The until-dark "best pass" notification still says "40° above the horizon".
  • A new player is told "23 of its 23 stars are new to you" when a constellation comes back.
  • Fleet alerts say "a new Starlink batch": the game's word is stamp.
  • Rare alerts call an Epic or a Legendary "a rare find".
  • Look up's title opens on an end time ("Until 9:02 PM: best viewing"), which reads like store hours.

Not recommended: snooze (a pass won't wait), a weekly push digest, Live Activities for single passes (maybe later for a cloud-break or shower night, which last hours).
