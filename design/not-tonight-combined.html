2 · How you get there

The page no longer opens on its own. The first time you open the app each day, this toast slides up and stays until you tap it (opens the page) or close it with ✕. It also waits until any card reveal has finished, so it never interrupts a catch. The radar's "Tonight" tab opens the same page any time.
