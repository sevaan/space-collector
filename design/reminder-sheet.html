Thinking it through. A visible pass lasts only 2 to 6 minutes, so “as it rises” is late for most people: by the time you get shoes on and find the direction, half of it is gone. 10 minutes suits the most common case (indoors: get outside, open the app, find the east). 5 minutes suits someone already near a door or on a balcony. As it rises is for when you’re already out with the phone. 15 minutes added little: you’d end up waiting outside. So three choices, defaulting to 10.
