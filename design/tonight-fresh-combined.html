Every night, all year, everywhere

The same page has to work in June in Scotland, in December above the Arctic Circle, at the equator, in the southern hemisphere and on every kind of bad night. Places and special nights below are worked out with the app's own planner and today's orbits (Real = tonight's actual data; Illustrative = the real shape of that night computed with today's orbits, so the exact satellites on future dates would differ). Weather and player states are what-ifs on tonight's Peterborough passes.

Needs your yes

  • "Tell me if it breaks" is a new notification: when the forecast for a dark hour with something worth catching turns clear. Counts as one of the two a day.
  • Clear-sky chance is 100 minus the forecast cloud for that hour (MET Norway). It's a guide, not a promise; we'd say "clear sky" not "chance of seeing".
