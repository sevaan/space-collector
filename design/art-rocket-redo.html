Rocket stage art: spent, tumbling, no flame

The craft room noticed about 30 rocket pictures share one diagonal pose with the engine firing, which is wrong for a stage that's just drifting in orbit. These 35 prompts redraw them in the same style, each from a different angle.

  1. Paste a prompt into your image generator (the one you used for the card art), with one or two existing card images attached as a style reference.
  2. Save each result as a PNG named exactly as shown (for example sl-8.png) into one folder.
  3. Tell me the folder. I run scripts/import-art.py, which makes the card and tile versions, and the cards pick them up.
